A cooked frosting, closer to a soft custard than a buttercream. The
crushed pineapple keeps it from setting hard, so the top of the cake
stays tender and a little glossy.

Have the frosting ready as the cake comes out of the oven. If the frosting
cools too much before it goes on, warm it back up on low heat so it pours
smooth.
