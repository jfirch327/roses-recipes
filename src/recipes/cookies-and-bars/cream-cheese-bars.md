Rose used to bring a pan of these to church suppers whenever it was her
week. The crust is a yellow cake mix pressed flat with a beaten egg and a
stick of melted margarine, and the filling is a soft cream cheese layer
poured right over the top before the whole thing goes in the oven for
about half an hour.

The filling sets while it bakes and stays tender once it cools. Let the
pan cool through before you cut into it, or the bars will pull apart on
you. Covered in the icebox, they keep for three or four days without
losing anything.
