A poke cake in the truest sense. The whole trick is punching holes across
the top while the cake is still hot, so the sweetened condensed milk and
cream of coconut sink all the way through and pool at the bottom.

Rose kept this one for summer suppers and family reunions. It travels
well in the pan it's baked in, covered with the lid or a piece of foil.
