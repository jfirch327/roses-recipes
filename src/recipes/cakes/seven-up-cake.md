The 7 Up is what makes this [cake](/cakes/) tender. The bubbles work the
batter the way beaten egg whites would in an older recipe, and the citrus
in the soda gives the crumb a little lift you wouldn't get from milk. If
you've spent any time with Rose's other [sheet cakes](/cakes/), you'll
recognize the shape of the method.

Rose always made this one with the pineapple pudding when she could find
it. Vanilla pudding does the job when the store is out. As soon as the
pan comes out of the oven, she'd pour the hot
[pineapple coconut cake frosting](/frostings-and-fillings/pineapple-coconut-frosting/)
right over the top so it drank the whole batch in. If you're setting a
table for a Sunday afternoon, this one belongs in the
[Sunday supper collection](/collections/sunday-supper/) alongside the
other cakes and the peanut butter pie.

It sits well overnight and travels covered. A pan of this shows up at
plenty of the recipes tagged for the
[potluck collection](/collections/potluck/), and it earns its spot at
every one.

**Cooks well with:** a scoop of the
[no-churn mocha ice cream](/puddings-and-custards/mocha-ice-cream/) on
top of a warm slice, or a cup of coffee on the porch. If you're keeping
track of the flour and eggs across recipes, the
[ingredient index](/ingredient-index/) lists everything Rose called for.
