The one recipe on this site that comes straight from the garden. Zucchini
peaks in August around here, and when the vines start giving you two or
three at a time, this is where they go.

The bread stays moist for days on the counter under a piece of foil, and
a loaf wrapped tight and frozen keeps for months. Rose used to send one
home with anybody who came by in September.
