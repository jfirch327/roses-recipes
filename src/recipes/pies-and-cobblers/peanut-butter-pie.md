ingredients:
  - group: "Peanut butter crumble"
    items:
      - "3/4 cup powdered sugar"
      - "1/2 cup peanut butter"
      - "One 9-inch baked pie shell"
  - group: "Filling"
    items:
      - "1/2 cup sugar"
      - "1/8 teaspoon salt"
      - "5 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon cornstarch"
      - "2 1/2 cups milk"
      - "2 egg yolks"
      - "2 tablespoons butter"
      - "1 teaspoon vanilla (see notes)"
instructions:
  - "Mix the powdered sugar and peanut butter in a small bowl until it turns crumbly. Press two-thirds of the mixture into the bottom of the baked pie shell. Reserve the rest for the top."
  - "In a saucepan, blend the sugar, salt, cornstarch, milk, egg yolks, and butter together."
  - "Cook over medium heat, stirring often, until the pudding thickens and starts to bubble."
  - "Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla."
  - "Pour the hot pudding over the peanut butter crumble in the pie shell."
  - "Sprinkle the reserved peanut butter crumble across the top."
  - "Let the pie cool on the counter, then chill in the icebox at least two hours before slicing."
notes: |
  Rose's card doesn't call for vanilla, but a teaspoon stirred in after
  the pudding thickens smooths it out. Add it if you like or leave it off
  to stay true to the card.

  Some cooks in the family top the pie with meringue or Cool Whip instead
  of the reserved crumble. Either way is fine. Rose used the crumble.
---

The heart of this pie is the layer of peanut butter and powdered sugar
crumble hidden under the pudding. As the hot filling settles over it,
some of the crumble melts into the pudding and some stays as a soft
band along the bottom of the crust.

A slice cuts clean once the pie has had a few hours in the icebox. Right
out of the oven it runs on you.
